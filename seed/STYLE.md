# seed/STYLE

The year is 2025-09-01 to 2026-09-03 18:40 (+01:00). It must read like two specific people, not like
sample data. Every screen in the app is designed and judged against it.

## The two people (see people.json)

**Noor** — art teacher. Types fast, lower case, drops apostrophes, abbreviates ("tmrw", "prob"),
uses "x" at the end of some messages but not all, sends photos of things rather than of herself,
asks questions she then answers herself, gets annoyed at admin, loves a list. Quiet 08:30–15:30 on
weekdays except a burst at 12:40–13:10. Alive in the evenings until ~23:30. Sends Mischief and
Sparkle most; Static when marking. Uses `nyeh`, `did_it`, `poke`, `thinking_of_you` a lot.

**Teo** — night-shift nurse. Three or four nights a week (19:30–08:00), then a flood at 08:15 on
the walk home, asleep until ~15:00. Writes in short complete sentences with full stops, sometimes
one word. Dry. Cooks on days off and reports on it. Sends Shelter and Warmth most; Ache on the
third night of a run. Uses `here`, `squeeze`, `steady`, `long_day`, `make_you_tea` a lot.

Their pronouns are hers and his; neither ever calls the other "babe"; they use names rarely and
"you" mostly. No pet names except "pigeon" once it exists.

## Rhythm rules (these make it real)

- Uneven days. Some days have forty messages, some have one feeling and nothing else, some have
  nothing at all. At least four silent stretches of three or more days in the year (Teo's runs of
  nights, Noor away at half term, Christmas apart, one unexplained).
- Conversations trail off. About a third of exchanges end without an answer. The last message in
  a day is often left hanging.
- Logistics dominate. Bins, keys, bread, dentist, roster, boiler pressure, whose flat tonight.
- In-jokes recur without explanation: the pigeon, tuesday soup, the left sock, the lift (from
  August), the good pen. Never explained in the messages; the reader infers.
- Feelings replace words. A Static feeling instead of a complaint; `here` instead of "I'm here";
  a `squeeze` at 03:10 with no message.
- Photos are of things: the soup, the pigeon on the cupboard, the canal, a marking pile, a key on
  a table, the lift buttons, bread, a receipt, a page of a sketchbook, the ward car park at 08:00.
- Voice notes are rare (three to six a month), mostly Teo walking home.
- Typos stay. Edits are rare and fix a real error ("thurs" → "tues"). Deletes are rarer (two a
  month at most): something sent to the wrong person, a message rethought.
- State: mood/availability/need/energy/place declared a few times a day between them, mostly
  passing (Teo → heads_down at 19:25 every shift; Noor → energy 1 during marking week).
- Dates are ordinary: the place by the bridge, the ferry, a film, a walk, a market, one wedding
  they attend together. Rated out of five with one-line notes that are honest ("cold. worth it.").
- To-dos are shared chores and errands, assigned both ways, some never done (reopened, ignored).
- Milestones: 2025-11-09 one year; 2026-02-02 first key; birthdays 03-14 and 07-27; 2026-07-18
  ferry (custom); a few "firsts" (first tuesday soup rated 10: never).
- Rituals begin in March 2026: "sunday 9 call" (Noor), "text when home" (Teo, after every night);
  `ritual_kept` lines are quiet and unremarkable, some weeks missing with no comment.
- Pings: authored by a person for the other: Noor sets "drink water" for Teo's nights at 02:00 in
  May; Teo sets "boiler pressure" fortnightly in January.

## Density targets (per month, roughly; vary them)

messages 180–350 · feelings 35–70 · reactions 20–40 · photos 4–10 · voice notes 3–6 · videos 0–2 ·
state_declared 60–120 · edits 2–6 · deletes 0–2 · date_event 2–8 lines · todo_event 6–16 lines ·
milestone 0–2 · ritual_kept (from March) 6–9 · ping 0–3 · feeling_authored (Oct, Feb only).

## Never

- No repeated messages, no filler ("hey", "how are you", "lol" as a whole message more than once a
  month), no obviously generated text, no explanations of the in-jokes, no emoji, no exclamation
  marks from Teo (Noor uses at most two in the whole year, and only in September 2025).
- No mention of the app, of "feelings" as a feature, of sending "a squeeze" — they just send it.
- No names of real places, brands, films, or people.
