# DIRECTION

The visual and interaction language, and every decision that shapes it. Read this before touching
anything under `app/lib/material/`, `blender/`, `assets/`, or `tools/handwriting/`.

## The one sentence

A note passed under a desk. Two people, one long thread of paper, lit by an afternoon window.

## The two people (invented; `seed/people.json`)

| | Noor | Teo |
|---|---|---|
| hand | fast, slanted ballpoint, blue-black; loops open; crosses t late | upright, rounded pencil, presses hard; graphite grey; letters sit heavy on the line |
| font | `NoorHand` (tools/handwriting, 5 variants per glyph) | `TeoHand` (tools/handwriting, 5 variants per glyph) |
| paper habit | tears strips off whatever is nearest: lined, graph, receipts | folds things: thirds, halves, corners turned |
| ink colour | ballpoint blue-black `#1f2a44`, red pen for logistics | graphite `#3a3a3c`, occasionally too-dark biro `#141a2e` |
| device | Android phone (host) | iPhone (PWA client) |

Neither person is "the user". The app never names itself and never addresses either of them.

## Light

One light rig for everything, forever: **soft daylight from a window up and to the left**.
- Key: sun disk, azimuth 315°, elevation 50°, warm (5200 K), strength low; large soft sky dome.
- Contact shadows fall **down and to the right**, short, warm-grey, soft-edged. Never a drop shadow.
- Recorded as `blender/rig/daylight.py`; every render imports it. Nothing else may add a light.
- **Dusk** is a second lighting *condition* on the same rig (`blender/rig/dusk.py`): the sky goes to
  a cool blue-grey at low elevation, and a desk lamp on the right, warm (2700 K), becomes the key.
  Night appearance = the dusk-rendered stocks and the lamp-baked shadows. Never a dim overlay.

## Palette (drawn from stationery, not from a UI palette)

- Paper: warm cheap-notebook off-white `#f1ecdf`; year-in-a-bag yellow `#ece0c2`; graph paper cool
  blue-grey `#e9ecec` with `#b9cbe0` rules; legal pad `#f3e6a8`; index card `#f6f1e6`.
- Ink: ballpoint blue-black `#1f2a44`; graphite `#3a3a3c`; hard-pressed biro `#141a2e`; red pen `#a8322b`.
- Accent: highlighter yellow `#f4ea6a` at 45% and pink `#f2a8c0` at 40% (multiplied, never on top);
  correction-fluid white `#f7f5ee`; aged tape amber `#d9b46b` at 55%; sticky notes `#f3e08a`,
  `#f2c1c1`, `#bcd8e8` faded.
- Nothing emits light. No glow, no neon, no gradient that is not the shading of a fold or curl.
- Partner state is carried by paper, ink and object choice, never by a coloured dot.

## Materials

All paper in the app is a render of modelled paper under the rig above, or a scan slot for one.
This container has no scanner and no physical paper, so **every "scan" in `assets/paper/` is a
Blender Cycles render of a modelled sheet**: fibre micro-displacement, tooth, printed rules with
misregistration, spiral fringe geometry, punched holes, edge aging. `blender/README.md` documents the
scan slots so the builder can drop real 1200 dpi scans into the same file names later; the app does
not care which it gets. This is the recorded assumption, not a shortcut: rendered geometry lit by one
rig is exactly what the fold and shadow pipeline needs, and it is the opposite of a CSS approximation.

- **Stocks** (`assets/paper/<stock>_<variant>.png`, 2400×3200 minimum): lined notebook, graph,
  spiral-torn-out, loose-leaf punched, legal, index card, three sticky-note colours; four aged
  variants of each notebook stock (rule offset, yellowing, warp differ). Dusk variant of every one.
- **Tears** (`assets/tears/tear_NNN.png`, alpha masks, ≥48): generated by `tools/tears/tear.py`, a
  fibre-pull tear model (a wandering fracture line with per-fibre pull-out and feathered fuzz), then
  relief-rendered in Blender so the torn edge catches the rig's light. `tools/tears/distinct.py`
  rejects any mask that is a flip, rotation, scale or crop of another (normalised cross-correlation
  across the D4 group and scales, threshold in `assets/MANIFEST.json`). The Chat capture log lists the
  mask ids on screen; no id may repeat within one frame.
- **Folds and crumples** (`assets/folds/<name>/NNNN.webp`): Blender cloth and shape-key sequences
  at 60 unique frames per second — `unfold_thirds` (240 f), `unfold_half` (240 f), `crumple_open`
  (120 f), `corner_curl` (60 f). Played through a rolling decoded-frame window; the WebKit texture
  budget is recorded in `TASK_STATE.md` and enforced by `tools/check/texture_budget.py`.
- **Handwriting** (`assets/fonts/NoorHand.ttf`, `TeoHand.ttf`, `DeskStamp.ttf`): built by
  `tools/handwriting/build.py` from authored stroke skeletons; each glyph has five variants with
  perturbed control points and a pressure profile that varies stroke weight along the path; the font
  cycles variants through `calt` so a repeated letter never repeats. Nothing is downloaded.
- **Feeling objects** (`assets/objects/<feeling>.png` + `_shadow.png`): folded paper shapes, tape,
  staples, stickers, pressed flowers, biro and pencil doodles; 3D ones rendered in Blender under the
  rig with their contact shadow baked from the render; flat doodles drawn by the stroke engine.
- **Tape, staples, clips** (`assets/bits/`): rendered pieces with baked shadows; ≥16 tape pieces.
- **Contact shadows**: always baked from the object's own render; the app composites object over
  shadow, never blurs a rectangle.

## Regions and the shell

Five regions, one paper desk: **Pulse · Chat · Us · Moments · Settings**. The shell is a desk seen
from above; each region is a different stack of paper on it. The region strip is a row of tabs cut
from index card, each with a handwritten label in DeskStamp. The partner-state strip (a torn strip
of the partner's current paper, in their ink, with their status line) sits at the top of every
region, so their state is legible everywhere. The feeling sender is a folded corner at the bottom
right of every region; one drag opens it; one release sends.

## Voice

`docs/VOICE.md` rules everything the app says. No app name, no exclamation marks, never "you, the
user". Empty states are written in the couple's own voice and drawn as the paper they would leave.

## Motion

Paper moves like paper: lifts (shadow grows), settles (shadow shrinks), curls at a corner before it
turns. Durations 180–420 ms with a slight overshoot. A feeling arriving is an object landing on the
desk: shadow first, object after, the sheet under it rocking once. On the PWA, where there is no
haptic, the **rhythm goes to the page**: the sheet lifts and settles in the exact haptic pattern, and
the feeling's paper sound plays the same rhythm. Same data, different body.

## Decisions log

- 2026-09-03 · Flutter + Dart single codebase; Android native + web build as the iOS PWA.
- 2026-09-03 · No scanner exists here: paper "scans" are Blender renders with documented scan slots.
- 2026-09-03 · One light rig, one dusk condition; light direction recorded above and in `blender/rig/`.
- 2026-09-03 · Two hands, five variants per glyph, contextual alternates; a third stamped face for labels.
- 2026-09-03 · Feelings: six families × five to six members = 33 built-ins (see `docs/FEELINGS.md`).
- 2026-09-03 · Spine storage: drift over sqlite3 (native on Android, wasm on web) inside `app/lib/spine/` only.
- 2026-09-03 · Transport: host (Android, tailnet-bound HTTPS) / client (PWA served by the host);
  ordering by host sequence; cursor sync; outbox on both sides; pairing = six-word code → HKDF key → HMAC.
- 2026-09-04 · A note lies on the desk and touches it. The contact shadow exists only because paper
  curls: `blender/paper/tear_relief.py` models the sheet touching in the middle and lifting along
  its torn edges, so the shadow is hidden under the paper that makes it and opens out around the
  break. A flat sheet under a small sun casts a slab, which is what a drop shadow looks like, and
  the first attempt produced exactly that.
- 2026-09-04 · The edge layer is a band, not a sheet. It carries only the two or three millimetres
  along the break where the fibres have been pulled open and the unprinted core of the paper shows;
  everything further in is the stock's own render and is never painted over.
- 2026-09-04 · Tear masks are assigned by position in the thread, not by a hash of the event id.
  A hash cannot promise that no two tears repeat on one screen — with 56 masks and eight notes it
  collided about a third of the time. Walking the pool with a stride coprime with its size makes a
  repeat within a screenful impossible rather than unlikely. The variation an id would have given
  is carried by the stock, the lift and the tilt instead.
- 2026-09-04 · Nothing counts. An unread count on the chat tab became a corner folded down on the
  card: it says the same true thing without keeping score, and a number that goes up while you are
  not looking is the mechanism the brief forbids.
- 2026-09-04 · No icon set anywhere. Where a word fits, the word is used, in the margin hand. Where
  a word will not do, the mark is a pen stroke with a hand's wobble and its pressure swelling in
  the middle of the stroke (`app/lib/material/marks.dart`).
- 2026-09-04 · Read markers and passive signals are derived from the seeded months rather than
  authored alongside them, and the validator rejects either one written by hand. Nobody types a
  battery level.
- 2026-09-04 · The home screen says "the other phone". The app still does not name itself, and on
  an iPhone the thing you tap is the person, not a product.
